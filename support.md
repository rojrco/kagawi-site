# Kagawi support

Something not working, or an idea to share? Write to [hello@rojr.co](mailto:hello@rojr.co). A real person reads every message.

## Common questions

**Do I need an account?**
No. Kagawi works fully on your phone without one. Sign in with Apple only if you want your habits on more than one device, or want to share a routine with friends.

**How do rest days work?**
Swipe a habit to the right on the Today tab and choose Rest day. A rest day keeps your streak going and never counts against you. Each habit can take up to 2 rest days per week, and they come back next week.

**How does Apple Health work?**
When you make a habit, choose "Apple Health" as the way to track it, then pick steps, calories, protein, carbs or fat. Kagawi reads your daily total and ticks the habit when you reach your goal. Kagawi only reads from Health and never writes to it. Your Health numbers stay on your phone. Friends only ever see that a habit was done.

**My steps or food are not showing up.**
Check that Kagawi is allowed to read them in the Health app (Sharing, Apps, Kagawi). If you log food in another app, make sure that app is set to share with Apple Health. New food can take a little while to arrive.

**How do I share a routine with a friend?**
Sign in first (You tab). Then, on the Habits tab, open a routine, tap Edit, choose "Share this routine", and tap "Send the code" to send your friend the 6-character code. They open the You tab, choose "Join with a code", and enter it. Each of you sets your own goals, and you only see what got done.

**How do I sign out?**
On the You tab choose Sign Out. You can keep your habits on the phone or remove them. Routines shared with friends always come off the phone when you sign out, and come back when you sign in again.

**How do I delete my account?**
On the You tab, scroll to "Delete your account". This removes your account and everything stored online (your name, your backup, and what you share with friends). You choose whether your habits stay on the phone or are removed too. It cannot be undone.

**I have a new phone.**
Sign in with the same Apple ID and your routines, habits and ticks arrive. Apple Health numbers are read fresh on each device.
