# Kagawi privacy policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Kagawi is run by JR Francisco ("we"). This page explains what Kagawi does with your information. The short version: Kagawi works without an account, your Apple Health numbers never leave your phone, there are no ads, and we do not track you.

Questions: [hello@rojr.co](mailto:hello@rojr.co)

## If you never sign in

Everything stays on your phone: your habits, routines, ticks, rest days and settings. We do not receive any of it.

Kagawi can read your steps, calories, protein, carbs and fat from Apple Health to tick habits that you set up that way. This is read on your phone only. Kagawi never writes to Apple Health, and these numbers are never sent to us or to anyone else.

Reminders are scheduled on your phone as local notifications.

## If you sign in with Apple

Signing in is optional. When you do, we store:

- **Your account:** a user ID, and the email address Apple gives us (this can be Apple's private relay address).
- **Your display name:** the name you choose, which Apple may fill in the first time. You can change it on the You tab.
- **A backup of your habits (on by default after you sign in):** your routines, habits, ticks and rest days, so another device you sign in on can show them. You can turn this off, or delete the cloud copy, on the You tab. Apple Health numbers are not part of the backup.
- **Your plan:** whether you have a free or premium plan.

## If you share a routine or join a group

- People you share with can see your display name.
- A shared routine includes its name, colour, symbol and its habits (names, descriptions, icons, colours and schedules). Goals on Apple Health habits are your own and are not shared.
- Friends see which shared habits you ticked on which days. They never see what you did not do, your Health numbers, or any rankings.
- A Cheer is a short message from one group member to another about showing up today. We store who sent it to whom, in which group.

## Who else handles your data

We use Supabase to host the account and data described above. Sign in with Apple is provided by Apple. We use no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services, and we do not sell your data.

## What we do not collect

Your Apple Health numbers, location, contacts, photos, browsing history, or advertising identifiers. Kagawi does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

## Keeping and deleting your data

- You can delete your account on the You tab ("Delete your account"). This removes your account and everything stored online for it, including your backup and your shares. You choose whether your habits stay on your phone.
- Routines you shared stop being shared with the people you shared them with.
- Our hosting provider keeps routine backups of its databases. Deleted data can remain in those backups for a short time before it ages out.
- To delete data without the app, write to [hello@rojr.co](mailto:hello@rojr.co).

## Children

Kagawi is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

## Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above. If a change is significant we will tell you in the app.
